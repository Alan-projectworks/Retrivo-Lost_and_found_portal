02 • RECOVERY ARCHITECTURE

Four Steps to Recovery

How Retrivo replaces scattered WhatsApp groups and chaotic search with a structured, verified return pipeline.

01

Report an Item

File a secure report for a lost or discovered item with category, campus zone, approximate time, and private identifiers.

02

Search the Portal

Our smart matching engine continuously checks newly reported items against current campus recovery records in real time.

03

Verify Ownership

Submit an ownership claim answering hidden verification questions. Campus staff conducts human review before any release.

04

Collect Belonging

Visit the designated collection desk (Library, Uni-Mall, Block 34, Hostels). Staff logs physical ID and completes the return.
