Report a Discovered Belonging

Thank you for helping reconnect a fellow student or staff member with their lost item. Please provide details while observing our campus privacy protection guidelines.

Campus Privacy Policy (Crucial):
If you found an ID Card, Wallet, or Official Document, do NOT post the student's full name, registration number, or internal card numbers in the public description. Retrivo will automatically safeguard sensitive items so only the verified owner can claim them.
For ID cards or wallets, use generic titles like "LPU Student RFID Smart ID Card" or "Men's Leather Wallet".
Keep private details out of here so claimants can prove ownership later.
This information is stored securely in the database and only shown to Campus Security / Staff to verify ownership claims.
We strongly recommend handing over found items to the nearest official campus security desk or library helpdesk.
Photos help students identify their items quickly. For ID cards, ensure the registration number is obscured.
